Equity principles for all tools

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
Compensate participationStipends, childcare reimbursement, and food lower barriers and signal respect. Build compensation into the engagement plan.
Never rely on one channelNo single tool reaches all communities. Combine in-person and digital, English and non-English, formal and informal settings.
Meet people where they areGo to the places, times, and formats that work for the community, not the other way around.
Use trusted partnersCBOs, promotores, and neighborhood leaders can reach communities the county cannot. Compensate them fairly.
Close the loop, alwaysEvery tool that collects input creates an obligation to report back through the same channels used for outreach.

Resources key: Low = minimal staff time and budget. Med = planned staff time, some budget. High = dedicated staff capacity, significant budget and coordination.
