Reaching underrepresented communities

The communities most often described as hard to reach aren't disengaged. They face barriers that standard outreach wasn't designed to address.

Updated August 28, 2026 at 1:59 PM PDT

Download PDF

Populations consistently underrepresented in Marin engagement

  • Youth and young adults
  • Seniors and older adults
  • Communities of color and historically marginalized groups
  • Limited English proficient individuals
  • People with disabilities
  • Low-income and economically burdened residents
  • Undocumented and mixed-status households
  • Agricultural and service industry workers
  • Long-term homeowners on fixed incomes
  • People with limited digital access or low literacy
  • People experiencing housing instability or homelessness
Best practice
  • Trusted partners, promotores, and community-based organizations already embedded in communities are among the most valuable engagement assets available. Partner early and compensate fairly.
  • Where budget allows, stipends, childcare, food, and transportation support aren't extras — they're what makes participation possible for the people who matter most.

Barriers and what works

BarrierWhat helps
Language accessQualified interpreters, translated materials, bilingual staff or partners
Caregiving obligationsOnsite childcare, stipends, virtual participation options
TransportationPop-up events closer to community, stipends
Work schedulesEvening and weekend meetings, recorded sessions, multiple touchpoints
Trust and historical harmLead with trusted partners, build relationships before the project begins
Cultural and social barriersFood, storytelling, partnerships with faith-based and cultural organizations
Fear and safety concernsAnonymous participation, minimize personal data collection, use trusted intermediaries
Disability accessADA-accessible venues, ASL, captioning, large print, advance materials
Digital access and literacyPaper surveys, phone options, community locations with internet access
Youth engagementSocial media, peer networks, school partnerships, contests, stipends
