Foreword

Marin County serves one of the most engaged and informed communities in California. The Public Engagement Plan (PEP) is the County's commitment to being clear with community members about how they can participate, what level of influence they have, and how their input will be used — engaging early, following through, and reporting back after every engagement effort.

Every staff member, vendor, or consultant who touches a project, program, or policy that affects the public is expected to use this Plan.

How to use this guide

This Plan is both policy and a practical reference. You don't need to read it cover to cover — jump to what you need:

If your project is already underway, start where you are: publish an Influence Statement, document input, and close the loop when engagement concludes. OCE Communications and the Office of Equity are your partners — reach out before you begin.
