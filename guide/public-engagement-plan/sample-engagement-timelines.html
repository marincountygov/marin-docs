Sample engagement timelines

There is no standard engagement timeline — history, language access needs, staff capacity, Board calendar, and trust all shape it. This table gives you a floor, not a ceiling.

These are minimum lead times under favorable conditions. Complex projects, hard-to-reach communities, multilingual engagement, and low baseline trust all require more time. Plan accordingly.

What it looks like in practice

A county planning department is redesigning a community park in a neighborhood with a significant Spanish-speaking population and a history of low participation in county processes. Staff spend four weeks in pre-engagement conversations with local CBOs and neighborhood leaders before anything goes public. Three bilingual listening sessions run at different times and locations, with food and childcare provided. A bilingual survey runs concurrently. Input shapes the final design priorities before the project goes to the Board. A bilingual What We Heard summary goes back to participants through the same channels used for outreach within 30 days.
