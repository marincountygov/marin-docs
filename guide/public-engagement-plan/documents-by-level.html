Required documents by level

Use this table after completing the Engagement Assessment and Plan to confirm your engagement level and required documents. OCE Communications must review the Plan before outreach begins at any level, and the completed Stakeholder Mapping Worksheet at Consult level and above.

LevelRequired documents
InformEngagement Assessment and Plan; Strategic Communications Plan
ConsultEngagement Assessment and Plan; Strategic Communications Plan; Stakeholder Mapping Worksheet; Influence and Outcomes Tracker; What We Heard Report
InvolveEngagement Assessment and Plan; Strategic Communications Plan; Stakeholder Mapping Worksheet; Meeting/Event Guide (if meetings are used); Influence and Outcomes Tracker; What We Heard Report; Evaluation
CollaborateEngagement Assessment and Plan; Strategic Communications Plan; Stakeholder Mapping Worksheet; Meeting/Event Guide (if meetings are used); Influence and Outcomes Tracker; What We Heard Report; Evaluation; Clerk of the Board consultation (if forming an advisory body)
EmpowerEngagement Assessment and Plan; Strategic Communications Plan; Stakeholder Mapping Worksheet; Meeting/Event Guide (if meetings are used); Influence and Outcomes Tracker; What We Heard Report; Evaluation; confirmed executive support; defined scope and decision authority in writing

Also include in every Board staff report

  • Engagement level used
  • Communities engaged
  • Summary of what was heard
  • How input shaped the recommendation

Once the engagement level is confirmed, continue with the Stakeholder Mapping Worksheet and the engagement planning section of the Engagement Assessment and Plan.

When the plan isn't followed

Accountability for engagement rests with the department initiating the project. OCE Communications and/or Equity is the countywide partner, not the enforcer. Staff reports must explain absent engagement documentation. Repeated departures from Plan requirements may require remediation before a project advances.
