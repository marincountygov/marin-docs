Description
The General Fund is the primary operating fund of the County and is used to account for all financial resources except those required by law or practice to be accounted for in another fund. It supports many of the essential County services such as health, safety and administration. Miscellaneous projects and countywide emergency response are accounted for in Fund 1010, which is a subset of the General Fund, and includes expenditures that are non-recurring and may span multiple years.
