Description
The Marin County Employee Retirement Association (MCERA) is an independent special district, governed by the Retirement Board. This County fund (2150) accounts solely for the salaries and benefits of MCERA employees. The MCERA Operations special district fund (7007) accounts for all other revenues and expenditures of MCERA.
