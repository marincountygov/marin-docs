Description
The Technology and Vehicle Replacement Internal Service Funds are general government funds used to administer the ongoing replacement of employee computers, communication devices and county-owned vehicles. Each department contributes to the funds equal to their allocation, based on the number of employees, equipment and/or motor vehicles. New equipment and vehicles are funded through the department’s existing appropriations and incorporated in ongoing replacement schedules in the following fiscal year.
