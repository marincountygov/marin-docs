Closing out

Closing a question ends new ideas, comments, and votes. People can still read the conversation.

Updated October 29, 2026 at 1:47 PM PDT

Don't let the engagement end without explaining what happened.

Closing the loop

A good closing recap answers three questions:

  • What did we hear? Share the main ideas and different views.
  • What did we learn? Explain what the feedback tells us.
  • What happens next? Share next steps, decisions, or when people can expect another update.

Review feedback

Look at the ideas, comments, votes, themes, and reports.

Identify common interests, different perspectives, and useful suggestions.

Share results

Publish a recap that explains:

  • What we asked. Remind people of the purpose.
  • Who participated. Share available participation information.
  • What we heard. Summarize common themes and different views.
  • What happens next. Explain how the County will use the feedback.
  • When to expect updates. Share the next milestone, if known.

If you created a campaign, close its questions and share an overall update.

Thank participants

Thank residents and community partners for their time and contributions.

The most important part of engagement is closing the loop. People should know their feedback was heard, even when the County cannot act on every idea.
