Why partners matter

Good engagement starts with trust.

Updated October 29, 2026 at 1:47 PM PDT

County announcements help spread the word, but trusted community partners can make the invitation more personal.

Partners may include nonprofits, neighborhood groups, service providers, schools, and other local organizations.

They help Engage Marin:

  • Reach more people. Connect with communities that County channels may miss.
  • Build trust. Invite people through groups and leaders they already know.
  • Encourage participation. Explain why the topic matters to their communities.
  • Support inclusion. Help people with different backgrounds and experiences take part.
  • Measure reach. Use partner links to understand how people found the conversation.

Partner early

Don't wait until your question is ready to publish.

Partners can help you understand what matters to their communities before you finalize the engagement.

Ask partners to help:

  • Identify issues and concerns you may have missed.
  • Review questions for clarity.
  • Suggest ways to reach people.
  • Share the invitation through trusted channels.
  • Encourage people to take part.

Engage Marin gives partners their own links, logos, and welcome messages. This lets them invite people in their own voice while connecting everyone to the same conversation.

Partners do more than share a link. They help turn a County question into a community conversation.
