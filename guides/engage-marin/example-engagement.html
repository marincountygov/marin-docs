Training example

These examples are for training only. They do not describe an active County project or actual engagement results.

Campaign

Title: Improving Marin parks

Description:

Marin's parks bring people together and offer places to play, relax, and enjoy nature. We want to learn how people use these spaces and what could make them better. Explore the questions below and share your ideas. Your feedback will help the County understand community priorities as it plans future park improvements.

Question

Question: How can we make County parks more welcoming?

Description:

County parks serve people of all ages and backgrounds. We want to hear what works well and what could improve. Share your ideas to help us understand what matters most to park visitors.

About page

Overview

County parks give people places to enjoy nature, spend time with family, and connect with their community.

We want to learn how people use these spaces and what could make them more welcoming.

Why we're asking

People visit parks for many reasons. Some come to walk or play. Others come to relax, meet friends, or enjoy nature.

We want to hear from people with different needs and experiences.

How to participate

Share what you enjoy about County parks and what you would change. You can also read other ideas, join discussions, and show support for suggestions.

What happens next

The County will review the feedback and look for common themes. What we learn will help inform future planning.

We'll share a summary of the results when the conversation closes.

First post

Welcome to the conversation!

County parks are important places for our community. They give us room to play, relax, and enjoy the outdoors.

We want to hear your ideas for making these spaces more welcoming.

What do you enjoy most about County parks? What could make your visits better?

Share your ideas, read what others have posted, and join the discussion. We welcome different experiences and points of view.

We'll follow the conversation and share updates as we learn more.

Thank you for helping us make Marin's parks better for everyone.

Partner invitation

Example message for a community partner:

We're working with Engage Marin to hear ideas about improving County parks. Your experiences and ideas matter. Join the conversation and help us understand what would make these spaces more welcoming for your community.

Progress update

Thank you for sharing!

We've received ideas about park access, trails, play areas, and places to gather.

We're reviewing what people have shared and looking for common themes.

There's still time to join the conversation. We welcome more ideas and different perspectives.

Closing recap

What we heard

Thank you to everyone who shared ideas about County parks.

Participants raised several common themes, including access, trails, play areas, and places to gather.

What we learned

People use parks in different ways. Their feedback helps us better understand what works and where improvements may help.

What happens next

The County will review these ideas as part of future park planning. We'll share more information as next steps become clear.

Thank you for helping shape the future of Marin's parks.
