About Engage Marin

Engage Marin is the County of Marin's online community engagement platform. It gives people a simple way to share ideas, give feedback, and help shape County decisions.

Updated October 28, 2026 at 4:36 PM PDT

  1. Plan

Engage Marin helps County agencies reach more people, hear different views, and learn what matters to the community.

You don't need to be an engagement expert to get started. The Engage Marin team can help you plan your approach, create content, and launch your conversation.
