First post

Every question should begin with a welcome post. This starts the conversation and shows that someone is listening.

Updated October 28, 2026 at 4:36 PM PDT

Limits

ContentLimit
First post75–150 words

Aim for 75–150 words.

Include:

  • Welcome. Thank people for joining.
  • Why it matters. Explain why the topic is important.
  • What we're asking. Reinforce the question.
  • Participation. Invite ideas, experiences, and questions.
  • Next steps. Let people know what to expect.

Tips:

  • Write like you're starting a conversation, not making an announcement.
  • Be authentic and conversational.
  • Focus on participants, not the County.
  • Welcome different perspectives.
  • End with an invitation to join the conversation.
