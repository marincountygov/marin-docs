How it works

Engage Marin helps you manage community engagement from start to finish. You can ask one question or bring several related questions together in a campaign.

Updated October 28, 2026 at 4:36 PM PDT

  1. Plan
  2. Create
  3. Partner
  4. Launch
  5. Monitor
  6. Organize
  7. Update
  8. Close
  1. Plan. Decide what you want to learn, who you want to hear from, and how you'll use their feedback.
  2. Create. Start a question or campaign. Add an About page, welcome video, and kickoff update.
  3. Partner. Work with trusted community groups to shape outreach and reach more people.
  4. Launch. Publish your engagement and invite people through County and partner channels.
  5. Monitor. Follow ideas, comments, votes, and participation.
  6. Organize. Set themes to group related ideas and understand what people share.
  7. Update. Post updates when appropriate to share progress and keep people informed.
  8. Close. End participation, review results, and publish a recap with next steps.

A successful engagement doesn't end when people stop commenting. It ends when you show them what you learned and how their input will help.
