Questions

A question is where people share ideas, comment, and vote.

You can create a standalone question or add it to a campaign. Each question has its own page, participation settings, updates, themes, and reports.

Writing the question

Your question is the most important part of the conversation.

Tips:

  • Short. Aim for 10 words or fewer.
  • Simple. Use plain language.
  • Focused. Ask one question.
  • Open. Invite ideas, not yes/no answers.
  • Neutral. Don't lead people toward a preferred answer.

Examples:

  • What transportation improvements would make the biggest difference for you?
  • How can we make County parks more welcoming for everyone?
  • What should we prioritize in next year's budget?

Question description

Use 2–4 short sentences to give people enough context to respond.

Include:

  • Why it matters. Explain why you're asking.
  • What you're asking. Tell people what feedback you're looking for.
  • How it will help. Explain how the County will use the feedback.

Tips:

  • Use plain language.
  • Avoid jargon and explain technical terms.
  • End with an invitation to participate.

Content builder

The Engage Marin content builder can help turn project notes into draft content.

It can help create:

  • Campaign titles and descriptions.
  • Questions and descriptions.
  • About pages.
  • First posts.
  • Welcome video titles and descriptions.

Use it to get started, then review the content with your team before publishing.
