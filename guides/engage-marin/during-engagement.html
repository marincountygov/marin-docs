During engagement

Once your question is live, people can share ideas, comment, and vote when those features are enabled.

Your team should stay involved.

Monitor activity

Use Engage Marin's Overview and Reporting tools to follow participation.

Look at:

  • Ideas submitted.
  • Comments and votes.
  • Participation over time.
  • Topics drawing the most interest.
  • Questions or concerns that may need a response.

Set themes

Use Themes to group related ideas and understand common interests.

Themes can help you see what people are saying across many responses. Review them as participation grows and adjust them when needed.

Post updates

Use Updates to keep people informed.

Share updates when:

  • The engagement reaches an important milestone.
  • You have new information to share.
  • Several participants ask the same question.
  • You want to encourage more participation.
  • The engagement is nearing its close.

A question that receives regular, useful updates shows participants that the County is listening.
