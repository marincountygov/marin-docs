What to expect

Engage Marin makes community engagement easier, but a successful effort still takes planning, outreach, and follow-through.

Updated October 28, 2026 at 4:36 PM PDT

  1. Plan
  2. Create
  3. Partner
  4. Launch
  5. Monitor
  6. Organize
  7. Update
  8. Close

Your team should plan to:

  • Create a clear question and explain why it matters.
  • Identify partners and plan outreach.
  • Follow participation and share updates.
  • Review feedback and identify common themes.
  • Report back when the engagement closes.

The goal isn't just to collect ideas. It's to listen, learn, and show people how their input matters.
