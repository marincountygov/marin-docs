Engage Marin Playbook

Start with a question. End with an answer.

Updated October 29, 2026 at 1:47 PM PDT

Engage Marin helps you invite community ideas, learn what matters, and share what happens next.

Your team brings the topic and community knowledge. The Engage Marin team helps you plan, launch, and manage the conversation.

The lifecycle

Every engagement follows this basic process, whether you ask one question or create a campaign with several questions.

  • 1. PlanDecide what you want to learn, who you want to hear from, and how you'll use their feedback.
  • 2. CreateStart a question or campaign. Add an About page, welcome video, and kickoff update.
  • 3. PartnerWork with trusted community groups to shape outreach and reach more people.
  • 4. LaunchPublish your engagement and invite people through County and partner channels.
  • 5. MonitorFollow ideas, comments, votes, and participation.
  • 6. OrganizeSet themes to group related ideas and understand what people share.
  • 7. UpdatePost updates when appropriate to share progress and keep people informed.
  • 8. CloseEnd participation, review results, and publish a recap with next steps.

Getting started

  • About Engage MarinEngage Marin is the County of Marin's online community engagement platform. It gives people a simple way to share ideas, give feedback, and help shape County decisions.
  • How it worksEngage Marin helps you manage community engagement from start to finish. You can ask one question or bring several related questions together in a campaign.
  • Your commitmentEngage Marin provides the tools and support. Your team brings the subject knowledge and stays involved throughout the conversation.
  • Why partners matterGood engagement starts with trust.
  • What to expectEngage Marin makes community engagement easier, but a successful effort still takes planning, outreach, and follow-through.
  • Ready to start?You don't need every detail worked out before reaching out.
