            About the data

            This tool reads the County's public Job Classifications and Pay Rates dataset (opens in a new tab). Pay ranges belong to job classes, not individual people. They do not include overtime, deductions, premiums, or individual benefit costs.

            Search, filtering, comparison, and exports happen in your browser. The public dataset is temporarily cached in your browser; your search and comparison choices are not saved. Pay rates and other data may change when the County updates the source dataset.
