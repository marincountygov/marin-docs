AIRFIELD-INSPECTION-2026

Airfield Inspection and Hazard Identification

Daily inspection and hazard-response procedure supporting safe airport operations.

Active procedure; approval and effective dates not provided in source

Overview

Identifier
AIRFIELD-INSPECTION-2026
Status
Active procedure; approval and effective dates not provided in source
Source organization
Airport Operations
Audience
Airport personnel responsible for daily airfield inspections, hazard response, NOTAMs, and follow-up
Expected result
Airfield hazards are identified, addressed or communicated, and tracked through corrective action
Topics
Airfield inspection, Hazard identification, Foreign Object Debris, Wildlife activity, NOTAMs

Conduct the daily inspection

1Inspect airfield operating areas

Conduct a daily inspection of runways, taxiways, aprons, lighting systems, signs, and safety areas.
Responsible role
Airfield inspector
Completion criteria
All listed operating areas and systems have been inspected.

2Check for hazards and operational issues

Inspect for Foreign Object Debris (FOD), wildlife activity, pavement damage, standing water during winter, obstructions, lighting issues, transient aircraft requiring tags, and conditions in hangar rows.
Responsible role
Airfield inspector
Required record
Daily inspection findings

Respond, notify, and follow up

1Address hazards immediately when possible

Correct or control identified hazards immediately when the condition can be safely addressed.
Responsible role
Airfield inspector

2Issue required NOTAMs

Issue NOTAMs for conditions that may affect aircraft operations.
Responsible role
Authorized airport personnel
Completion criteria
Affected aircraft operators can receive timely notice of the condition.

3Notify Airport Management

Notify Airport Management of significant safety concerns.
Responsible role
Airfield inspector

4Track corrective action to completion

Follow up on identified hazards until corrective action is completed.
Responsible role
  • Airfield inspector
  • Airport Management
Completion criteria
The corrective action is complete and the hazard no longer affects safe operation.

Responsibilities

Airfield inspector
Conducts daily inspections, identifies hazards, addresses conditions when possible, issues or initiates required notifications, and follows up.
Airport Management
Receives significant safety notifications and supports corrective action.

Documentation and records

Record type
Airfield inspection and corrective-action records
Requirement
The source requires inspection and follow-up but does not state a retention period.
