Purpose

Guide County staff, designers, and vendors in applying the County of Marin logo consistently, while allowing appropriate departmental and agency expression.

  • Promote a uniform portrayal of the County.
  • Create continuity across departments and agencies.
  • Improve efficiency in producing stationery and other materials across County government.

Background & significance

The main graphic element is a drawing of the Marin Civic Center designed by Aaron Green, Frank Lloyd Wright's West Coast Representative and Architect of Record for the Marin Civic Center.

The symbol represents:

  • transparent governance
  • environmental preservation
  • healthy, sustainable and safe communities

Logos

  • Use supplied artwork; identity art should never be altered or recreated.
  • Do not stretch or distort logos or logotypes.
  • Provide enough breathing room around logos and do not overlap them with other logos or graphics.

Color palette

Black and gold. Black is used for line art and typography; gold derives from the Civic Center spire and is used in special cases.

Six additional colors derived from the building or surrounding environment. Blue should be used sparingly, primarily as an accent. Grays may be used for lighter logo screens; remaining colors may be used as larger backgrounds or in graphics such as charts and graphs.

Use of the palette is intended to create continuity and a strong family brand across departments and agencies.

Typography

  • Futura is the primary font used in Marin Civic Center signage and was adopted for official County logo and logotype art.
  • Arial Regular is the preferred font for body copy.
  • Georgia may be substituted when a serif font is needed.
  • Verdana is identified in the 2014 guide as the website font.

On MarinOS digital products

MarinOS digital products (this site included) use Jost for headings rather than Futura or Verdana. marin-digital-standards/brand/typography.md sanctions this explicitly: on modern digital systems, County hierarchy is preserved through scale, weight, line-height, and spacing rather than forcing the exact 2014-guide typeface. Futura remains reserved for official logo/logotype artwork itself, which is never re-set in another face.

Heading
Jost (marin-ui shared bundle)
Body
System sans-serif stack (Arial, Verdana, system-ui fallbacks)

File formats

County of Marin identity art should never be altered or recreated.

Print
EPS — Vector-based format for high-resolution print applications. (PMS spot colors, CMYK). Vector artwork can be enlarged without losing quality.
Web & electronic
PNG — For web, on-screen and Microsoft Office applications; selected for line-art quality, smaller size and transparency support. (RGB).
